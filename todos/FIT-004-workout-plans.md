# FIT-004: Provide workout plans and an exercise library

**Status:** Complete  
**Priority:** P0  
**Depends on:** FIT-002, FIT-003

## User story

As a member, I want a workout structure suited to my goal and experience so I
know what to do when I train.

## Scope

- Define a safe, maintainable exercise catalogue with target muscles,
  instructions, sets/repetitions, rest guidance, and regression options.
- Create an editable weekly plan using the member's goal, experience, and
  available training days/equipment.
- Provide clear guidance that plans are general fitness information, not
  medical advice; account for injury limitations by recommending professional
  guidance rather than diagnosing.

## Acceptance criteria

- Members can view their plan and exercise instructions on mobile and desktop.
- Plan generation uses validated profile inputs and has useful empty/error states.
- Exercises include actionable form and safety cues.
- Recommendations avoid implausible promises or one-size-fits-all medical claims.
