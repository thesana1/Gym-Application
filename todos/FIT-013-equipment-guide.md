# FIT-013: Browse equipment tutorials

**Status:** Complete  
**Priority:** P2  
**Depends on:** FIT-004

## User story

As a member, I want clear instructions for common gym equipment so I can
understand its purpose and use it more confidently.

## Scope

- Provide an equipment guide for the equipment types already covered by the
  exercise library.
- Include primary use, target muscles, step-by-step instructions, common
  mistakes, safer/easier alternatives, and safety notes.
- Reuse reviewed exercise-library instructions rather than maintaining duplicate
  exercise guidance.
- Defer video embeds until a vetted, appropriately licensed demonstration
  source is selected.

## Acceptance criteria

- Equipment types are searchable/filterable from an authenticated dashboard
  page.
- Equipment pages show only exercise instructions present in the local library.
- Safety guidance is visible and does not imply medical advice or guaranteed
  safe form.
- No unverified third-party videos, ratings, or equipment claims are presented.

## Delivered behavior

- Added a searchable, filterable equipment guide for dumbbells, resistance
  bands, resistance machines, and barbells.
- Equipment setup and general safety notes are paired with matching exercises
  from the existing library, including their target areas, instructions, common
  mistakes, easier variations, and exercise-specific safety cues.
- Added the guide to protected dashboard navigation.
- Video demonstrations remain out of scope until an appropriately vetted and
  licensed source is selected.
