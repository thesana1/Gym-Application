# FIT-006: Set nutrition targets and track daily intake

**Status:** Complete  
**Priority:** P1  
**Depends on:** FIT-002, FIT-003

## User story

As a member, I want to see daily nutrition targets and log meals so I can make
informed choices that support my stated goal.

## Scope

- Define nutrition target and food-entry types for calories, protein,
  carbohydrates, fat, and fiber.
- Let users enter their own targets; do not infer medical or individualized
  nutrition prescriptions from the current profile fields.
- Allow daily meal entries and water tracking with edit/delete actions.
- Explain that food values are user-entered and may be estimates; provide a path
  to qualified advice for users with medical or dietary needs.

## Acceptance criteria

- Members can record, edit, and review their own daily intake.
- Daily totals are calculated from saved entries, with units clearly shown.
- User-entered targets and food values are not represented as medical advice or
  independently verified recommendations.
